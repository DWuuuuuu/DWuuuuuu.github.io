Problem 259 · Conditional probability

The painted cube

A 3 × 3 × 3 cube is painted red on the outside and cut into 27 unit cubes. Choose one uniformly at random and roll it. The top face is red. What is the probability that you picked a corner cube?

Solution 1 · Count painted faces

Each cube and each orientation is equally likely, so each of the 54 painted unit faces is equally likely to be on top given that the top is red. Eight corners contribute three painted faces apiece. The probability is 24/54 = 4/9.

Solution 2 · Condition on the observation

A corner is chosen with probability 8/27 and shows red on top half the time. Across all cubes, 54 of 162 unit faces are painted, so a red top has probability 1/3. Bayes’ rule gives (8/27 × 1/2) ÷ (1/3) = 4/9.
